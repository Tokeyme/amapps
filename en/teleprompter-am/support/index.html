Frequently asked questions

Where are my recordings?
In the app under “Recordings” of each script — and as video files in the Files app under “On My iPhone” › “Teleprompter”. With “Save to Photos” you also put a copy in the Photos app. If you delete the app, the recordings inside it are gone; the copy in Photos stays.

How does the text follow my voice?
Tap the voice symbol in the prompter. The app listens through the microphone and moves the text to the place you are speaking — also after a pause or when you skip a sentence. Recognition runs on the iPhone only; nothing is transmitted. It works for English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Italian, provided iOS has the language pack on your device. If it is missing, the app says so; iOS usually downloads it by itself.

The text scrolls, but not at the right speed.
Without voice following, the text scrolls at a fixed speed in words per minute; change it with the arrows at the bottom, with “+” and “−” on a keyboard, or with a page turner. A tap pauses, a swipe jumps within the text.

The app says the iPhone is getting hot.
Camera and speech recognition together are demanding for older iPhones. The recording goes on; if it gets too hot, the app stops and saves it cleanly. A break or unplugging the charging cable helps.

Picture and sound are out of sync.
This happens with Bluetooth microphones. In the prompter settings under “Recording”, the audio offset moves the sound against the picture; plus makes it later, minus earlier. The setting applies to new recordings.

Which resolution and frame rate can I choose?
Whatever your front camera can do — the app offers only that. An older iPhone, for example, 720p and 1080p at 24 to 60 frames. Each recording shows in the list what it was made with.

How do I use the app with a beam-splitter teleprompter?
In the prompter settings: camera preview off and “Mirror horizontally” on. For screens lying upside down there is “Mirror vertically”.

How do I change the language?
The app follows the language of your iPhone. To change it for this app only, go to the iOS Settings under “Teleprompter” › “Language”. The language of a script is detected from its text; in the editor you can set it explicitly.

Will I lose my scripts and recordings when I switch iPhones?
Not if you back up your iPhone via iCloud or a computer and restore the new one from that backup. If you delete the app, though, the data is gone.
