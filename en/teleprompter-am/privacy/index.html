Privacy Policy for the app “Teleprompter AM: for Video”

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the iPhone app “Teleprompter AM: for Video” (shown on the home screen as “Teleprompter”, the “app”) handles your data. It applies to this app only, and not to websites, app stores or third-party profiles.

1. Controller

The controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Andreas Maier
Dürerstr. 14, 73547 Lorch, Germany
Email: support@amapps.app

2. No data protection officer

No data protection officer under Art. 37 GDPR has been appointed, because there is no legal obligation to do so (single individual, no large-scale processing of personal data). Please contact the controller above with any questions.

3. Overview

“Teleprompter AM: for Video” is a teleprompter with video recording that runs entirely on your iPhone. The controller runs no servers of his own and does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

  • There is no registration, no user account and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking components, no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no crash reporting to third parties. No usage profiles are created.
  • The app makes no network connections. What you write and record stays on your iPhone only.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores on your iPhone:

  • Your scripts (title and text), including earlier versions and, after deletion, 30 days in the app’s trash.
  • Your recordings as video files in the app’s Documents folder. They are visible in the Files app under “On My iPhone” › “Teleprompter”, so you can reach your videos even without the app.
  • Your settings, such as font size, speed, resolution, frame rate and the chosen microphone.

This data is kept inside the app’s own area and is not transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your iPhone via iCloud or a computer, iOS includes the app’s data in that backup; Apple or your computer is responsible for it, not the controller of this app.

5. Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera and microphone only when you start a recording yourself or turn on voice following. iOS asks for your permission first; without it, both stay off. Picture and sound are written only to the video file on your iPhone. A level meter shows how loud your voice is; it stores nothing.

6. Speech recognition (“Follow my voice”)

On request, the app makes the text follow your speech. For this it uses the speech recognition of iOS on the device only: the app sends no audio to Apple or anyone else. Only what is being said right now is recognized, to find the matching place in the script; the recognized text is not stored. iOS asks for your permission first. The language packs this needs are downloaded by iOS itself; that happens under Apple’s responsibility and outside this app.

7. “Save to Photos”

Only when you choose it, the app places a copy of a recording in your Photos app. For this it asks for permission to add photos; it does not read your existing photos. What happens to the copy afterwards (for example syncing via iCloud Photos) depends on your settings and Apple’s terms.

8. Sharing, export, import

Through the iOS share sheet you can pass on recordings, export scripts as text or import text files. Where a video or text goes is your decision; the recipients and the apps involved are governed by their own privacy terms. Imported files are read on your iPhone only.

9. Legal basis

To the extent any processing takes place at all, it serves the performance of the contract of use of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). As the controller receives no personal data from you, he cannot provide information about you or erase data. Everything the app stores is on your iPhone and under your control alone.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data, whatever the user’s age.

12. Apple as distributor

The app is obtained through the App Store. In doing so, Apple processes data under its own responsibility (for example about the download). The controller has no influence on this and receives no knowledge of it. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app changes, this policy will be updated. The version published here, with the date stated above, is the one that applies.
