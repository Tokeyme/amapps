Common questions

Will I lose my game when I quit?
No. Entscheider saves continuously and picks up exactly where you left off on the next launch, whether you quit the app (on Mac with Command-Q) or close the window. On Windows, closing the window (X at the top right) or Alt+F4 quits the game. You can also create saved games of your own with the folder button in the bar; there are three slots.

Why does it take a while for my character to reach another room?
That is intentional. The character walks through the corridor first. Entscheider is meant to be played calmly and gives you time to think.

How is the game scored?
By company value, after one, four and eight weeks, in your own high-score list. After 25 February 1994 you may keep playing; it is no longer scored then.

How do I turn the music down or off?
With the radio button in the bar. There you choose the station, set the volume or switch the radio off.

Can I make the window bigger?
Yes. On Mac as big as you like, including full screen via the View menu; on Windows with the Maximise button, by double-clicking the title bar or with Win+Up arrow (there is no full-screen mode of its own there). It cannot be made smaller than 1024 × 600 pixels, so everything stays readable.

Where do I get Entscheider?
On Mac from the Mac App Store, for Android from Google Play, and for Windows from the Microsoft Store (listed there as “Decision Maker 1994” or, in German, “Entscheider 1994”) or on Steam (listed there as “Decision Maker”, or “Entscheider” in German, free of charge).

Where are my saved games, and how do I delete them?
On your own device; we do not receive them. On Mac they are kept in the app’s own area under ~/Library/Containers/com.entscheider.app, where you can delete them. On Windows they are kept in the file shared_preferences.json: with Steam in the folder %APPDATA%\Andreas Maier\Entscheider\, in the Microsoft Store version in the app’s package folder (enter %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages in the address bar of File Explorer and open the folder whose name begins with AndreasMaier.Entscheider1994_; the file is within it at LocalCache\Roaming\Andreas Maier\Entscheider\). Uninstalling from the Microsoft Store makes Windows remove this folder; with Steam it remains after uninstalling, so delete the file or the folder by hand. On Android you delete the data in your device settings under Apps → Decision Maker → Storage, or by uninstalling the app. For details, including the Android backup with Google, see the privacy policy.
