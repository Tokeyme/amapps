Common questions

Will I lose my game when I quit?
No. Entscheider saves continuously and picks up exactly where you left off on the next launch, whether you quit with Command-Q or close the window. You can also create saved games of your own with the folder button in the bar; there are three slots.

Why does it take a while for my character to reach another room?
That is intentional. The character walks through the corridor first. Entscheider is meant to be played calmly and gives you time to think.

How is the game scored?
By company value, after one, four and eight weeks, in your own high-score list. After 25 February 1994 you may keep playing; it is no longer scored then.

How do I turn the music down or off?
With the radio button in the bar. There you choose the station, set the volume or switch the radio off.

Can I make the window bigger?
Yes, as big as you like, including full screen via the View menu. It cannot be made smaller than 1024 × 600 pixels, so everything stays readable.
