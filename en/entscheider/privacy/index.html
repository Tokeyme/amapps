Privacy Policy for the app “Entscheider”

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app “Entscheider”, called “Decision Maker” in English (the “app”), handles your data. It applies only to the macOS version from the Mac App Store and the Android version from Google Play (package name app.amapps.decision), and not to websites, app stores or third-party profiles. The browser version at decision.amapps.app and on itch.io has its own privacy policy.

1. Controller

The controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Andreas Maier

Dürerstr. 14, 73547 Lorch, Germany

Email: support@amapps.app

2. No data protection officer

No data protection officer under Art. 37 GDPR has been appointed, because there is no legal obligation to do so (single individual, no large-scale processing of personal data). Please contact the controller above with any questions.

3. Overview and principle

“Entscheider” is a business simulation game that runs entirely on your Mac or your Android device. The controller runs no servers of his own and does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

  • There is no registration, no user account and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking components, no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no crash reporting to third parties. No usage profiles are created. For statistics and crash reports that Apple and Google compile themselves, see section 8.
  • The app makes no network connection of its own. It downloads nothing and sends nothing; even the game’s music is included in the app. The Android version’s own code does not even request permission to access the internet (for a check that Google adds, see section 8).
  • Your saved games and settings stay on your device, and the controller does not receive them. On Android, your own device backup with Google may also include them (see section 4).

4. Data stored locally

The app stores on your device:

  • The game in progress, so the next launch continues exactly where you left off.
  • Up to three saved games of your own, if you create them.
  • Your high-score list with the results of your games.
  • The radio settings (such as station and volume).
  • Depending on the version, also the game language you chose and a marker for gameplay hints (for example whether you have already found the way out of the boss’s office).

For playback, the app also keeps the music tracks in its own cache. None of this contains personal data, and the app does not transmit any of it anywhere.

Mac: The data is kept in the app’s own area on your Mac (~/Library/Containers/com.entscheider.app), where you can delete it at any time.

Android: The data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access. You can delete it under Settings → Apps → Decision Maker → Storage (or “Storage & cache”) with “Clear storage” or “Clear data” (the labels vary between devices), or by uninstalling the app. If you have turned on device backup in your Google account, Android may include your saved games and settings in that backup with Google and restore them after a reinstall; Android may likewise carry them over when you transfer to a new device. This is your own backup, governed by your settings and Google’s terms; the controller receives nothing from it. You manage and delete that backup in your Google account. Android does not include the music cache in the backup.

5. Legal bases

Where any processing takes place at all, it serves the performance of the contract for use of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The backup via your Google account (section 4) is governed by Google’s terms, and obtaining the app (section 8) by those of Apple or Google. For Google Play’s automatic protection, see section 8 as well.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

Since the controller receives no personal data from you, he can neither provide information about you nor erase any data. Everything the app stores is on your device (on Android, possibly also in your own backup with Google) and under your control. For data that Apple or Google process under their own responsibility, please contact them.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data, whatever the age of the user. The age ratings in the stores (which differ by store and country, often 3+) only assess the game’s content.

8. Apple and Google as distribution channels

You obtain the Mac version through the Mac App Store and the Android version (which has no in-app purchases) through Google Play. In doing so, Apple or Google processes data under its own responsibility (for example to download and update the app). The controller has no influence over this. Apple and Google provide him with aggregated statistics (such as the number of downloads per country) and, if you have agreed to share diagnostic data, crash reports (such as device model, OS version and the point of failure) that he cannot link to any person. Reviews you post in the store are public; he sees them there under the name with which Apple or Google displays them, and may reply to them publicly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; legitimate interest: answering questions and feedback about the app). The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply.

For the Android version, Google Play’s “automatic protection” is also turned on. It is meant to prevent modified copies of the app from being distributed. To this end, Google adds a check of its own to the app, which uses Play Integrity to determine whether the app came unmodified from Google Play. Google provides this check; it runs under Google’s responsibility, possibly through Google Play services on your device. Whether and which data is sent to Google in the process is determined by Google. No server of the controller is involved, and he receives no data about you from it. Insofar as the controller is jointly responsible for this check being turned on, he relies on his legitimate interest in preventing the distribution of modified copies (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object to this (Art. 21 GDPR).

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes, this policy will be adjusted. The version published here, bearing the date given above, is the authoritative one.
