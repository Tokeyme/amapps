Privacy Policy for the app “What am I cooking?”

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the iPhone app “What am I cooking?” (shown on the home screen as “Cookbook” or its name in your language, the “app”) handles your data. It applies to this app only, and not to websites, app stores or third-party profiles.

1. Controller

The controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Andreas Maier

Dürerstr. 14, 73547 Lorch, Germany

Email: support@amapps.app

2. No data protection officer

No data protection officer under Art. 37 GDPR has been appointed, because there is no legal obligation to do so (single individual, no large-scale processing of personal data). Please contact the controller above with any questions.

3. Overview

“What am I cooking?” is a recipe app that runs entirely on your iPhone. The controller runs no servers of his own and does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

  • There is no registration, no user account and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking components, no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no crash reporting to third parties. No usage profiles are created.
  • The app makes no network connections. All recipes and the ingredient catalog are included in the app.
  • Everything you enter stays on your iPhone only.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores on your iPhone:

  • Your own recipes with ingredients and steps, if you create any.
  • Your pantry, i.e. the ingredients you marked as available.
  • Your shopping list with amounts and ticked items.

This information is kept in a file inside the app’s own area and is not transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your iPhone via iCloud or a computer, iOS includes the app’s data in that backup; Apple or your computer is responsible for it, not the controller of this app.

When you use “Copy list”, the app puts the shopping list as text on your iPhone’s clipboard. What you do with it afterwards, for example pasting it into a message, is up to you.

5. Legal basis

To the extent any processing takes place at all, it serves the performance of the contract of use of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

As the controller receives no personal data from you, he cannot provide information about you or delete data. Everything the app stores is on your iPhone and under your sole control.

7. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and collects no data, whatever the age of the user.

8. Apple as distributor

The app is obtained through the App Store. In doing so, Apple processes data under its own responsibility (for example for the download). The controller has no influence on this and receives no knowledge of it. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes, this policy will be updated. The version published here with the date above applies.
