Frequently asked questions

How do I enter what I have at home?
In the Pantry tab: tap the ingredients in the categories or search for them at the top. The Ideas tab then shows what you can cook right away and where only one or two ingredients are missing.

Why do salt, pepper, oil and water always count as available?
Almost everyone has these staples at home. If you had to enter them, hardly any recipe would show up as ready to cook.

How do I add my own recipe?
With the plus button in the recipe list. Write one ingredient per line, for example “200 g flour” or “2 eggs”. Mark ingredients you can do without with “(optional)”. Below the field the app shows how it reads each line.

The app doesn’t know an ingredient.
No problem: it is shown as “new” and kept as you typed it. If you add the same ingredient to your pantry, it still counts. Feel free to tell us which ingredient is missing.

Can I change an included recipe?
Yes. Tap the pencil in the recipe. The original stays as it is; your own version is saved.

How do I share the shopping list?
Tap “Copy list” at the top of the shopping list and paste the text into a message.

How do I change the language?
The app follows your iPhone’s language. To change it for this app only, go to the iOS Settings, open the app and choose “Language”. Recipes and ingredient names switch along; your own entries stay as you wrote them.

Will I lose my recipes when I change iPhones?
Not if you back up your iPhone via iCloud or a computer and set up the new one from that backup. If you delete the app, however, the data is gone.
