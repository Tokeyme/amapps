Common questions

Why won’t a song play?
Most likely it isn’t on the device but only in your iCloud library, and hasn’t been downloaded there. The app tells you so when you tap the song. Download it in Apple’s Music app and it will play.

How do I get my own music files into the app?
Tap + at the top right. There are four ways: choose from Files, via AirDrop, share from another app, or by cable from a Mac.

Why does one album appear twice — or two albums only once?
Music AM merges the same album from several sources into one tile. A mark in the corner shows it was merged; inside the album you can undo it with Separate. The other way round: if something that belongs together was not merged, please write to us — those cases help us.

Can I download music from inside the app?
No. Apple offers no interface for that; downloading is only possible in Apple’s own Music app.
