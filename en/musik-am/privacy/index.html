Privacy Policy for the app “Music AM”

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app “Music AM” for iOS (the “app”) handles your data. It applies to this app only, and not to websites, app stores or third-party profiles.

1. Controller

The controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Andreas Maier

Dürerstr. 14, 73547 Lorch, Germany

Email: support@amapps.app

2. No data protection officer

No data protection officer under Art. 37 GDPR has been appointed, because there is no legal obligation to do so (single individual, no large-scale processing of personal data). Please contact the controller above with any questions.

3. Overview and principle

“Music AM” is a music player for files on your device and for your library. The controller runs no servers of his own and does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

  • There is no registration, no user account and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking components, no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no crash reporting to third parties. No usage profiles are created.
  • The app makes no network connection of its own at all. It downloads nothing and sends nothing.
  • Your music, your settings and whatever the app remembers stay on your device only.

4. Access to your library

So that the app can show and play your library, it asks for the Media & Apple Music permission. This permission is not requested on first launch, but only when you tap the corresponding button yourself. You can withdraw it at any time in your iPhone’s Settings; the app then continues to work with the files you brought in yourself.

Reading the library happens locally on the device through Apple’s interface. No data is transmitted to the controller in the process.

One note for completeness: if you play a track that is not stored on the device but in your iCloud library, playback is handled by Apple’s Music component, which then connects to Apple. This happens inside the operating system and not through this app; Apple is the controller for it. For files stored on your device, no connection whatsoever is made.

5. Data stored locally

The app stores on your device:

  • Music files you bring in yourself. They are kept in the app’s own storage and never leave the device.
  • The area you last opened, so the app reopens there.
  • Which albums you have separated, so that a merge you undid stays undone.

Nothing more. None of this contains personal data and none of it is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it with the app.

6. Legal bases

Where any processing takes place at all, it serves the performance of the contract for use of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Access to your library takes place on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time with future effect.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

Since the controller receives no personal data from you, he can neither provide information about you nor erase any data. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control alone.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data, whatever the age of the user.

9. Apple as the distribution channel

The app is obtained through the App Store. In doing so, Apple processes data under its own responsibility (for example about the purchase or download). The controller has no influence over this and gains no knowledge of it. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes, this policy will be adjusted. The version published here, bearing the date given above, is the authoritative one.
