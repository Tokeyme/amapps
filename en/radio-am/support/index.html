Frequently asked questions

Why do I only see the stations of one country?

That is deliberate. Radio AM brings each listener the stations of their own country instead of being a world shelf to browse. Which country applies normally follows your device.

How do I switch to another country?

Under “About” → “Country”. There is also “Automatic”, which makes the app follow your device again.

My station is missing. Can I add it myself?

Yes. In the “Stations” area, tap the plus at the top right, enter a name and the stream address, and save. The station then sits under “Your stations” next to the others. You usually find the address on the broadcaster’s website.

A station will not play

First check your internet connection and tap “Try again”. If it still fails, the broadcaster has probably changed its stream address — write to us and we will add the new one.

Can I record or download programmes?

No. Radio AM records nothing and downloads nothing — deliberately and permanently.

Playback stops when I close the app

It should keep going. Check in the iOS settings whether Radio AM is allowed background activity, and whether Low Power Mode is on — that sometimes ends background playback.

What does the app cost?

Nothing. No advertising, no account, no subscription, no in-app purchases.

What does the sleep timer do?

Set it in the player: after 5 to 90 minutes playback switches itself off.
