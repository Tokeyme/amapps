Radio AM

Listen to radio. Nothing else.

Radio AM plays the radio stations of your country — ad-free, without an account, without a subscription. Open the app, tap a station, listen. Nothing else happens.

Coming soon. The app is built and ready for review; once it is on the App Store, the link will be here.

What it does

  • Ad-free. No spot before the stream, no banner, no interruption.
  • No account. No sign-up, no email address, no registration.
  • Curated by hand. Every stream address comes from an official source of the broadcaster itself — public service and commercial alike.
  • Your own stations. If one is missing, enter its address yourself.
  • Favourites and background playback with lock screen controls.
  • Sleep timer from 5 to 90 minutes.
  • No data collection. No server, no tracking. The app connects to the stations — and to nothing else.
  • No recording. Radio AM records nothing and downloads nothing. Deliberately and permanently.

Where it is available

Radio AM shows the stations of the country you are in — if you live somewhere else, change the country in the app. Sixteen countries at launch:

GermanyAustriaSwitzerlandNetherlandsBelgiumDenmarkSwedenNorwayFranceItalyGreeceTürkiyeLatviaHungaryRomaniaCzechia

The interface speaks twenty-one languages and follows your device.
